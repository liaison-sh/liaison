---
title: Write and send a proposal
description: "Have an agent draft a proposal from your past work, set the price yourself, send it for signature and keep the priced part as a quote."
---

An agent can write the first draft of a proposal from the work you have already done. A person sets the price, and a person sends it. This page shows the steps and the limits.

## Draft the text

1. Keep notes on past projects in [Knowledge](../apps/knowledge.md). Mark them verified when they are right (see [Keep documents verified](../apps/knowledge.md#keep-documents-verified)).
2. Ask the agent for a proposal for a client: "Draft a proposal for Aurora Sound Works, a field-recording kit and training, using what we did for similar studios." It reads what you can read in Knowledge and writes the text.
3. Read it. Clauses an agent wrote are marked as drafts until a person reads them and saves.

## Set the price

An agent never sets a price. A person sets it on the agreement's page, and a proposal without a price cannot be prepared. The price you set is frozen into the version you send, so what the client signs is what you priced. See [Draft an agreement](../apps/agreements.md#draft-an-agreement).

When the proposal needs lines, quantities and tax, create a quote in Invoicing, which an agent drafts and a person approves (see [Send quotes and follow-up documents](quotes.md)). The proposal and the quote can link to the same company and deal, and a quote converts into an invoice when the client accepts.

## Send it for signature

Open the proposal in [Agreements](../apps/agreements.md), prepare it, name the signers and send it. Your own click sends it. An agent's send waits for your approval. The client signs on a page they open from the email, or from their [portal](../apps/client-portal.md). The signature is a simple one, with proof you can keep and check offline; see [What a signature here is](../apps/agreements.md#what-a-signature-here-is).

## What it does not do

- It does not choose or round a price, or invent terms from nowhere. It writes from what is in your Knowledge and what you tell it.
- It does not publish a document outside your organization. Send a document by mail or as a PDF, or share it through the portal.
- A proposal is not a quote. The priced lines are a quote, and the agreement holds the text and the signature.
