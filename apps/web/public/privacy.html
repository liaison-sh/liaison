Liaison privacy policy

Applies to the Liaison apps (iOS, Android, desktop and web) and to every Liaison server. Last updated 28 September 2026.

Liaison is workspace software. Each Liaison server is run by an organisation (your employer or a company you work with), and that organisation decides what data goes into it. In this policy, "the operator" means the organisation that runs your Liaison server. The operator is the data controller for your account and your work data; questions and requests about them go to the operator.

What the app itself keeps on your device

  • The address of the Liaison server(s) you added, and your sign-in session for each.
  • If you allow notifications: a push token issued by Apple or Google, sent to your server so it can notify you.
  • If you turn on biometric unlock: nothing biometric leaves the device. Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint checks are done by the operating system, which only tells the app "yes" or "no".
  • If you use the encrypted mailbox: your mailbox keys, encrypted on the device.

The app contains no advertising, no third party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites. Its developers receive nothing from it: there is no telemetry sent to anyone other than the server you connect to.

What a Liaison server processes

A Liaison server stores what you and your colleagues put into it to do your work: your account (name, email address, role), messages and threads, tickets, contacts, files, calendar and mail you choose to connect, and records of actions taken (an audit log). It may send text to an AI model provider chosen by the operator to answer your requests. Where the server runs, how long it keeps data and which providers it uses are the operator's choices; ask the operator for its own notice.

Notifications

Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, and can be shown on a locked screen. So the server strips them at the source: a notification about anything that requires a permission to read carries no text at all (no content, title, sender or thread name), only a generic wording, the link to open, a badge count and its type. Only messages that anyone in the workspace may read can include a short title and preview.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn notifications and biometric unlock off at any time in the app or in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account from the account settings ("Delete my account"). This erases your account on that server; the operator may keep records it is legally required to keep.
  • For access, correction, export or any other request about your data, contact the operator. Where the GDPR applies, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit (TLS). The encrypted mailbox, when used, encrypts mail on your device with keys the server does not hold.
