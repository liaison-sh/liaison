The client portal lets people of a client company read what you share with them and sign, accept or approve what you send them. How to invite them and open shares is on the [Contacts page](contacts.md#give-a-client-company-its-portal). This page is about the roles and the data.

## Who is responsible for what

- **Your organization** decides who of a client company gets access, what each company sees, and the notice its people read. For the personal data of those people (names, email addresses, sign-ins, what they read and decided), your organization is the one responsible for it.
- **Whoever runs the server** holds that data for you: you, when you host Liaison yourself, or the provider of the hosted edition. It processes the data only to run the portal for you.
- **Your client's own customers.** When a project of yours handles personal data your client is responsible for (their users, their customers), you may be acting as your client's processor for that work. Whether a data processing agreement is needed between you is a question for your lawyer. If you make one, the [Agreements app](agreements.md) keeps and signs it as a DPA; its starter data processing addendum, in English and French, is a starting point to review with your lawyer.

Access is an access rule, not encryption: whoever runs the server can reach the data.

## What the portal records

- **Notice.** Your notice shows to each person at their first sign-in and again after you change it, with **I have read it**. Write it to say who you are, what you record and why.
- **Access log.** Sign-ins, views, downloads and decisions, kept 12 months. Another length, between 6 and 36 months, needs a reason, kept beside the setting. Only the people given the access log permission read it, and it names them.
- **Data export.** A person's data export holds their portal account, its access log and the requests sent to them.
- **Ending access** signs everyone of the company out and closes their links and shares at once; invoices and signed documents are kept.
- **Erasure** of a portal person keeps what they signed, accepted or approved, and their company's invoices, and says which records it kept.

No agent, chat or search reaches a portal person.
