Handling a ticket from a VIP or high-value customer:

1. Confirm VIP status from the linked contact (tier or flag) before treating it as an escalation — do not assume from tone alone.
2. Acknowledge fast and personally; set a concrete expectation you can actually keep, not a heroic one.
3. Escalate by assigning to the right owner and flagging priority — do NOT promise a refund, discount, or delivery date you cannot authorize.
4. Anything that commits money or policy (refund, credit, exception) is GATED — propose it for human approval; never tell the customer it is done.
5. Keep the customer's contact and history in view so the reply reflects their real relationship, not a generic template.
