Regulation (EU) 2026/101 of the European Parliament and of the Council

on the labelling of connected devices sold in the internal market.

Article 1

This Regulation lays down rules on the information that manufacturers of connected devices shall make available to consumers before purchase, including the minimum period during which security updates are provided.

Article 2

Manufacturers shall state the support period on the packaging and on the product page in a clear and legible manner. The obligations apply from 1 January 2027.

Note on the consolidated text: this version includes the corrigendum of March 2026.
