## What's new

- The app now opens on Liaison Cloud: type your workspace name to sign in, or create a workspace. If your team runs its own server, choose a self-hosted server instead.
- Knowledge pages save themselves and keep a page history. Two people editing different parts of a page at once are merged.
- Type / in a page for blocks such as to-dos, checklists and callouts, link pages with @ or [[, and add images and files.
- Import your Notion export into Knowledge, and restore deleted pages from Trash.
- Routines gather their work into one request, which you can approve from Home.
- More of the app reads in French.
